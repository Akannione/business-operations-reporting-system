Business Problem

The organization has useful operational data, but the data is not decision-ready. Request records are duplicated, categories are inconsistent, dates are mixed, and managers do not have a weekly view of backlog, rework, service speed, or automation opportunities.

Business Value

The system turns disconnected spreadsheet-style data into a repeatable management reporting workflow. A team can use it to spot bottlenecks, reduce rework, improve intake forms, and prioritize automation where it actually saves time.

Workflow Built

  1. Generated a realistic messy operations dataset with duplicate records, inconsistent labels, missing values, and mixed date formats.
  2. Cleaned and standardized the data using Python, including categories, dates, boolean fields, and numeric values.
  3. Loaded the cleaned dataset into SQLite and created SQL queries for KPI summary, weekly metrics, department bottlenecks, channels, and request-type value.
  4. Generated dashboard-ready CSV outputs, an HTML dashboard, and a business-facing executive summary.
